Trainer's Tips
  • Tick the small checks, not a whole topic at once. The bold line is only a heading. The counter on it is the real progress.
  • Learn Excel first, then Power BI. Power BI reads the same kind of table. Cell references, filters, and pivots are the foundation.
  • Practice on the files in this repo. Open the datasets folder, do Phase A in Excel, then Phase B in Power BI. Items marked practice are the ones those guides use.
  • In Power BI, keep the order: Get data → clean in Power Query → model (including a date table) → measures → visuals.
  • Three mistakes that show up in the guides: dates left as text, a rate written as a column instead of a measure, and averaging a percent instead of dividing the sums.
  • Do not memorize every function. Learn what each family is for (lookup, conditional sum, date parts). You can look up the exact arguments.
Suggested Learning Order

Excel 1 → 2 → 3 → 4, then 5, 6, and 7, then 8 (PivotTable) and 9. Then Power BI 1 → 2 → 3, then 4 (do the date table before you trust any time measure), then 5 and 6, then 7. Use Hide done to see only the small points still open. Search when you come back to one skill.
